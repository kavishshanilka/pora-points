# 🔥 AURA POINTS - University Roast & Backfire Leaderboard

> **"Land a savage roast: +1 mark! If your roast fails and you get clowned: -1 mark!"**  
> The ultimate campus scoreboard for university squads, hostel gangs, and canteen squads to track who has the supreme roaster aura and who keeps choking their roasts!

---

## 🎮 Game Rules

1. **Awarding Marks (+ Marks)**:
   - When someone delivers a clean, savage roast on a friend: **`+1 Roast`** (or `+2`, `+5`).
   - Add a small note describing what roast landed!
2. **Deducting Marks (- Marks / Backfire)**:
   - When someone tries to roast their friend, but **badly fails**, everyone laughs at them, and they get roasted in return: **`-1 Backfire`** (or `-2`, `-5`).
   - Add a small note describing how they choked or self-burned!
3. **The Rankings**:
   - 👑 **#1 Roast God**: Highest marks (landed the most savage roasts and avoided choking).
   - 🤡 **Chief Backfire Victim**: Lowest marks (tried to roast people and constantly got cooked instead).

---

## ✨ Features

- 📝 **Attach Notes to Every Mark**: Add a custom description or pick from hilarious campus presets (*"Tried to roast GPA but forgot his own was lower"*, *"Dead silence followed his punchline"*, *"Clean canteen comeback"*).
- 💬 **1-Click WhatsApp Standings**: Formats a message with ranks, scores, and recent notes ready to paste in your batch WhatsApp group.
- 📸 **Story Card Poster Generator**: Generates and downloads an aesthetic dark-neon 800x1000 PNG poster for WhatsApp Status or Instagram Stories.
- 🔊 **Built-in Sound Synthesizer**: Native Web Audio API sounds (*Triumphant chord* on +1 Roast, *Sad cartoon wah-wah trombone* on -1 Backfire).
- 🔄 **Live URL Sync**: Share your board URL so friends can view and sync the same scores on their phones.
- 📱 **Mobile & PWA Ready**: Installable to Android / iOS home screen with one tap.
